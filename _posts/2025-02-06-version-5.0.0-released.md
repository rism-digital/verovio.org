The first version of Verovio in 2025 is now out as version 5.0. This is also the first version of Verovio supporting MEI 5.1 for both reading and writing.

One important feature in this new version is the support for scoring up mensural notation. This new feature was implemented by Martha E. Thomae with support of the NFDI4Culture with a grant to Anna Plaksin.
