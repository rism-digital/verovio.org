Many small contributions have been integrated into release 6.4, mostly related to the MusicXML importer and MIDI output. They illustrate the widespread adoption of Verovio for these tasks.

The release also includes TypeScript definitions as part of the NPM package. These have been available for a while but were previously distributed separately. Including them directly in the package will make them easier to maintain.

Regarding MEI support, `parts` and `part` are now supported structures, with each part rendered as a separate system.
