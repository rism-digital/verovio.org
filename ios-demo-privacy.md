### Verovio MEI Viewer Privacy Policy
*Effective Date: 14 July 2025*

**Verovio MEI Viewer** ("the App") is a mobile application designed to allow users to view and interact with MEI (Music Encoding Initiative) files. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

#### 1. No Data Collection
We do **not** collect, store, or share any personal data or user-generated content. The App functions entirely on the device and does not transmit data to any servers.

#### 2. No Third-Party Services
The App does not use any third-party services (e.g., analytics, advertising, crash reporting) that collect user information.

#### 3. No User Accounts
The App does not require users to create an account or log in.

#### 4. Local Access Only
Any files or content you view using the App remain on your device and are not accessed or transmitted elsewhere.

#### 5. Children’s Privacy
Since we do not collect any information, the App complies with privacy requirements for all users, including children under the age of 13.

#### 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy in the future. If we make any material changes, we will update the date at the top of this policy.

#### 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: [info@rism.digital](mailto:info@rism.digital)
